## THE FIVE LESSONS (~4 min each)

### L1 · Document your processes first 🎛️ lower-third: 1 · DOCUMENT FIRST
- We used to do ride-alongs and envisioning workshops.  Map how people actually work.  It was
  never just documentation | it was consensus building.
- **What's changed: AI.**  Upload your SOPs, have an agent gap-fit them against Microsoft's
  Business Process Catalog.  Hours, not weeks.  No SOPs?  Record the workshops, transcribe,
  ask AI to draft the SOPs and read the sentiment in the room.
- 🎙️ **Try this:** run one workshop this week, record it, and ask AI for the SOP draft + the
  sentiment.  Technical exercise AND team-building exercise in one.
- 💬 "Who still has zero written processes?  Be honest, this is a safe garage."

### L2 · Data quality is everything 🎛️ lower-third: 2 · DATA QUALITY
- KPIs are worthless on dirty data.  You can waste 10% of your techs' time on bad data alone.
- Don't start with KPIs.  Start with business outcomes, then pick the KPIs that prove them.
- **Your technicians are smart.  They will game whatever you measure.**  Choose carefully |
  reward what you want more of, watch one or two things you want stopped.
- Dynamics has the semantic layer built in.  Shape data right on day one and insights are free.
- 💬 "What's the dumbest metric you've ever been told to hit?  I'll share mine."

### L3 · Change management is not optional 🎛️ lower-third: 3 · CHANGE MANAGEMENT
- The hardest part isn't technology.  **Field Service is not ERP | there is no weekend cutover.**
  You're running a live service business while you change it.
- The failure mode: IT + leadership design it, zero service people in the room, big-bang rollout
  to the field.  Almost never goes well.
- **The pod approach:** small pilot team, new tools, let them outperform the old way in public.
  The org adopts what it can see winning.  Or start bottom-up with the painful old processes
  nobody will miss.
- 🎙️ Real change is small, measured steps with visible wins.  Not rip-and-replace.
- 💬 "Anyone lived thru a big-bang go-live?  How'd that weekend feel?"

### L4 · Don't turn on RSO too early 🎛️ lower-third: 4 · RSO: NOT YET
- The most expensive mistake I see.  Scheduling is deeper than everyone thinks, and RSO is not
  a checkbox | it's a different way of scheduling.
- Dispatchers are rightly skeptical: it's non-deterministic, different answers every run.  Trust
  is the feature you have to ship first.
- 🎙️ **The test from last week stands: can your team schedule a day well by hand?**  No?  Then
  the engine can't save you.
- Tease: full RSO deep-dive episode is coming.  *(EP03)*
- 💬 "RSO on, off, or scared of it?  One word in the chat."

### L5 · Choose your partner wisely 🎛️ lower-third: 5 · PARTNER FIT
- More important now than ever.  **AI is disrupting the partner channel** | discovery, docs, and
  data work that took months now takes days, and the big partners are feeling it.  Delivery
  team turnover is real: the people who start your project may not finish it.
- Don't lock into one big multi-year contract.  Tiered approach: one senior, experienced person
  (MVP-class) guiding strategy, multiple partners per phase if needed.
- 🎙️ **Focus on who does the work, not the logo.**  Ask hard questions about team continuity
  and knowledge handoff.  The power is shifting back to the customer.
- 💬 "Partners watching: push back.  I'll take the fight live."
