## THE FIVE QUESTIONS (the spine · ~3-4 min each)

### Q1 · Did you fix the process first? 🎛️ lower-third: 1 · FIX THE PROCESS
- Most do it backwards.  Buy Dynamics to clean up a messy process.  It won't.  You just
  automated the mess | now the mess runs faster.
- War beat: walked in six months post go-live, system doing exactly what they asked.  What they
  asked for was broken on day one.
- 🎙️ **Try this:** before you configure one thing, map how a work order actually moves today.
  Not the process doc | reality.  Can't draw it on a whiteboard in ten minutes?  Not ready.
- 💬 "Drop a 1 if you bought before you mapped.  Be honest, nobody's watching.  Well | we all are."

### Q2 · Do your techs actually want this? 🎛️ lower-third: 2 · WIN THE FIELD
- Most change-resistant group you'll meet, and I say it with love.  Didn't bring them along?
  They go around it.  Paper in the truck.  Texts to dispatch.  A shadow spreadsheet.
- War beat: a beautiful build, quietly ignored by the field for a full year.
- 🎙️ **Try this:** find your most skeptical senior tech | the grumbler.  Put them on the project
  early and actually listen.  Win that person, you win the field.  Steamroll them, you lose.
- 💬 "Techs watching | what's the workaround you're still running?  Call it out."

### Q3 · Did you switch on RSO too early? 🎛️ lower-third: 3 · WALK BEFORE YOU OPTIMIZE
- RSO is powerful and the fastest way to blow up the project if the foundation's soft.  Demos
  great, so people flip it on.  Garbage underneath | RSO just makes bad calls faster, with
  more confidence.
- 🎙️ **Try this:** before you even think RSO, ask | can my team schedule a day of work well by
  hand?  If humans can't do it manually, the engine can't do it for you.
- 💬 "Who turned RSO on in the first ninety days?  How'd that go?"  *(this is your EP03 teaser)*

### Q4 · Would you trust your own data? 🎛️ lower-third: 4 · TRUST YOUR DATA
- Everybody underestimates how bad their data is.  Everybody.  Wrong addresses.  Skills nobody
  touched since 2019.  Territories drawn by someone who left.  Bad in, bad schedules out | then
  people blame the software.
- 🎙️ **Try this:** pull fifty work orders and fifty resource records at random.  Read them.
  Embarrassed by what you find?  That's your real project.  Fix data before you fall for features.
- 💬 "What's the worst dirty-data story you've got?  Winner gets my sympathy."

### Q5 · Does your partner know Field Service, or just Dynamics? 🎛️ lower-third: 5 · PARTNER FIT
- Not the same thing.  A Gold partner can crush sales and finance and never have run a field
  operation.  Field Service is its own animal | scheduling, mobile, inventory in a truck, a tech
  with one bar of signal.  Never lived it?  They build demo-right, field-wrong.
- 🎙️ **Try this:** ask them to walk you through a real field day, dispatch to debrief.  Tell it
  cold?  Keep them.  Get vague?  Wrong partner.
- 💬 "This one gets spicy.  Agree or fight me in the comments."

## 3 · GUT CHECK | four warning signs (2 min) 🎛️ lower-third: WATCH FOR
Say you're already in one of these right now.  Four signs you're in trouble:
1. Your team keeps saying "we'll figure that out later."  Later never comes.
2. The consultants don't get the day-to-day reality of your techs.
3. Leadership keeps pushing go-live when the team clearly isn't ready.
4. You're spending more time configuring the system than fixing your actual process.
- 🎙️ Nodding at any of those?  Pump the brakes today.  So much cheaper to slow down now than to
  rescue this in six months.  Rescuing broken projects is how I make a living | I'd still rather
  you never need to call me.
