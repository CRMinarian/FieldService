## Notes
- **Channel keywords ≠ video tags.**  The block above is the channel-level setting; per
  video, pull 10-15 relevant tags from the bank plus 2-3 specific to that episode's topic.
- **Video tags matter less than title/description/thumbnail** for ranking — treat them as
  a tiebreaker, not the main lever.  Put the real SEO work into the title and first two
  lines of the description.
- Keep the brand terms (`Field Service Nerd`, `Pierre Hulsebus`) on every video so the
  channel builds branded-search equity over time.
