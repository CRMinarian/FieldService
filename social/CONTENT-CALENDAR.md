## Operating principles (channel-growth best practices)
1. **Rhythm over volume.**  One long video per week, same slot: **Tuesday 10:00 AM ET.**
   Viewers subscribe to a pattern.
2. **Seed quietly, launch loudly.**  Back-catalog migrations upload with notify OFF, straight
   into playlists.  New episodes get the full cross-platform push.  Never dump.
3. **Shorts are discovery.**  Two per week, cut in Descript from that week's episode, one
   "Try This" question each.  They feed The Honest Take.
4. **Every video echoes on LinkedIn.**  Tue 8:30 AM announce post; Thu 8:30 AM standalone
   value post (never two announcements in a week).
5. **Produce one week ahead.**  Grok interview + script by Wednesday, my asset package by
   Friday, record on the weekend, publish Tuesday.
6. **First 4 episodes get maximum effort** (per CHANNEL-PLAN.md) | they set the channel's
   baseline with the algorithm.
