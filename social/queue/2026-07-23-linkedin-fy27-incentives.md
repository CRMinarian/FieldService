## Post copy

Microsoft didn't cut partner incentives for FY27.  It replaced a floor with a ceiling.  Those are very different things, and almost nobody is saying it out loud.

Here's the confusion.  One camp is posting that the core incentive on Modern Work and Dynamics 365 went to zero.  The other camp, mostly distributors, is posting that you can now earn up to 19.5%.

Both are true.  Neither one is honest by itself.

The old number was small, flat, and guaranteed.  No adoption required.  No growth required.  Just volume and a pulse.  That was a floor.

The new number is large, conditional, and stacked.  You earn it by moving customers to premium products and growing year over year.  That is a ceiling, and most partners will never touch it.  "Up to 19.5%" is the maximum for a partner clearing every accelerator.  Reading that as your expected rate is like quoting a mutual fund's best year as your return.

So here's the accurate sentence.  Microsoft moved the money from the middle of the channel to the top of it.

The median partner will earn less.  The top quartile will earn more.  That isn't a budget cut.  It's a sorting mechanism.

And there's a second half nobody leads with.  In October, margin drops another five percent on the legacy standalone stack.  E1.  E3.  Exchange.  SharePoint.  Microsoft 365 Apps.  That one isn't conditional.  Your existing book gets less valuable on a schedule whether you grow or not.

Carrot in July.  Stick in October.

I lived this once already.  When we pushed partners to move to cloud, the short term math looked terrible and the long term math was transformational.  The partners who moved early built practices that still exist.  The ones who defended the old model until it was too late mostly never recovered.

So try this, this quarter, before you need it.  Pick your three best customers and build the before and after numbers now.  Utilization.  First time fix.  Seats grown.  You cannot retrofit a baseline, and under this model the baseline is the whole game.

And if you're on the customer side of this, ask your partner one question.  How does your team get paid this year?  Not because the answer disqualifies their advice.  Because you should know that renewals stopped paying and upgrades started.

Getting ahead of it is cheap right now.  Catching up later is not.

Have a super day.
