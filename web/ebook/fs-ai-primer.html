How to read this

This is a practitioner's field guide, not a vendor deck. Every claim is written from the perspective of someone who has shipped — and rescued — Dynamics 365 Field Service in production.

Read it in order or jump to the section you're fighting with today. Each section ends with a decision you can actually take back to your team.
